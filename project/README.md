The creator brand of **Hossam Gouraan · حسام جعران**: a long-term media asset built around one person and a **100-episode weekly journey**. Business is the home territory, and it may extend into work, people, systems, technology, AI, money, career and decisions. The brand is built in four layers. Only the first two are permanent.

| Layer | What | Lifespan |
|---|---|---|
| **01 Master identity** | Name, photography, colour, type, the Field, the Index Tab, the Square unit, the 100 Journey | Permanent |
| **02 Master content language** | Thumbnails, the Mark, video graphics (talk / evidence / journey), motion, short form, social, covers, web | Permanent, reused across all 100 |
| **03 Season layer** | The slot any season fills: name lockup, one gesture, Sxx prefix | Rule is permanent; contents change |
| **04 Season 01 · تكسير المسلّمات** | The Strike gesture, S01 prefix, lockup, season markers | Episodes 01–20 |

**The key test:** remove تكسير المسلّمات, the Strike and every S01 marker, and every piece must still read as Hossam Gouraan. The *Key test* card proves it.

Source of truth: `master-content-strategy.md`, `audience.md`, `voice-guide.md`, `youtube-philosophy.md`, `distribution-principles.md`, `production-capabilities.md`, and the Season 01 decision files. Section numbers below (§) refer to the Master Content Strategy unless another file is named.

## 01 · Master identity

### Positioning: intentionally unwritten
- **Don't put a tagline on master assets.** The strategy calls its hypothesis "not a permanent identity statement" (§26) and says the answer to *why people listen to Hossam* must be earned through the work (§27).
- **Role line:** `BUILDER · OPERATOR · RESEARCHER` (§6), set in mono as metadata. Never "coach", "expert" or "guru".
- **Relationship to portray (for scripts and layouts, not a printed line):** "I have been working on this question. Let's understand what is actually happening."
- **Editorial behaviour (master, every season):** سؤال → بحث → فهم → سياق → طريقة تفكير أحسن (Question → Investigate → Understand → Contextualise → Build a better way to think, §4).
  - Breaking assumptions is Season 01's way into the first step. It is not the brand.

### Voice
- Write in **natural Egyptian Arabic, plus the professional English Hossam would actually say**: workflow, system, AI, bottleneck, framework (Audience §8; Voice §4). No artificial MSA.
- Keep it regionally understandable across the Gulf.
- Use diagnostic questions, context before judgement, experience first and label second.
- No manufactured stakes ("الفيديو ده هيغير حياتك", "99%", "السر").
- No AI-language: «في عصر…», «لا شك أن…», «ليس X بل Y».
- No emoji in designs.
- Titles are statements, not literal questions. This is a shared default taken from the Season 01 research.

### Colour: locked
- **The core:** `brand-blue` #2047F4, `brand-navy` #0A1748, `black` #000000.
- **Tonal steps only:**
  - Blues: `brand-blue-deep`, `brand-blue-bright`, `brand-blue-soft`, `brand-blue-mist`
  - Off-blacks: `ink-900`, `ink-800`
  - Greys: `grey-700` / `500` / `400` / `200`
  - Lights: `paper`, `white`
- **No other hue exists**, not even for states, charts or seasons.
- **Pairs:**
  - White on blue (6.4:1), black (21:1) or navy (16.9:1).
  - One highlighted word: `brand-blue-bright` on black/navy, black on blue.
  - Never small blue text on black, and never navy on blue.

### Typography
- **Thmanyah Sans** (Black 900 / Bold 700) is for Arabic L01–L02 headlines only: thumbnails, season titles, hero, statement and question cards. Never use it for body, UI, metadata, citations or paragraphs. The Serif cut is excluded.
- **IBM Plex Sans Arabic** covers Arabic subtitles, body, captions and labels.
- **Archivo** (800, width 87.5 %) covers English display and every large numeral.
- **IBM Plex Sans** covers English text.
- **IBM Plex Mono** is the index voice: `04 / 100`, `SOURCE`, `@GOURAAN`.
- **Arabic** is right-aligned, never letter-spaced, never justified. Display line height 1.08–1.2; text 1.6–1.85.
- **Numbers:** Western digits, two digits for episodes.

### Name
- **Signature:** حسام جعران + the **Square unit**, a blue square of 0.2 em. The square means *one unit of the journey* (one episode, one question), not "final word" (§6).
- **English:** "Hossam Gouraan" in Archivo. In mono metadata: HOSSAM GOURAAN.
- **Short form:** @gouraan / GOURAAN■.
- **No logo, no monogram.** The avatar is always the face.

### Photography
- **Uniform:** black crew-neck T-shirt.
- **World:** a blue backdrop with a hard beam of light.
- **Portraits are real:** never mirrored, reshaped or smoothed.
- **Crops go by head size:** headshot 62 %, chest-up 42 %, waist-up 28 %.
- **Cut-outs** sit only on flat `brand-blue` and always meet the bottom edge.
- **Set bridge (flag):** the V1 recording set is a home set lit with softboxes and warm practicals (Production §6–7). A blue wall or paper backdrop behind the chair is an *optional production upgrade*, not an assumption.

### Master graphic devices (all rectangles, radius 0)
1. **The Field (primary):** a flat blue or black block meeting the photo at a hard vertical seam. Splits are 56/44, 100 or 33/67. Type sits on the Field, never on a face.
2. **The Index Tab (secondary):** a mono label at the top-right of the Field.
   - Master content is the **journey count `04 / 100`**. It is also used for card types (SOURCE, TABLE, سؤال) and carousel counters (02 / 06).
   - A season adds a prefix: `S01 · 04 / 100`.
   - **Extra content outside the 100** (technical builds, tools, experiments, §21) carries no count.
3. **The Mark (supporting):** a solid block behind the one word that carries the idea. Blue on black/paper, black on blue.
   - Max one per piece.
   - It also serves as the in-video keyword and the evidence highlighter on documents.
4. **The Square unit (micro):** the signature square. It also forms bullets and grid cells.

### The 100 Journey
- **The grid:** 20 columns × 5 rows, one row per 20-episode horizon (§12).
- **States:**
  - On dark: blue = published, white = this episode, grey outline = ahead.
  - On blue ground: black / white / deep-blue outline.
- **Where it goes:** banner, website, episode card, end card, milestone posts (10 / 20 / 50 / 100). Never on thumbnails.
- **Companion episodes** share their parent's square.
- **Seasons** may label their row but never recolour it.

## 02 · Master content language

- **Grid:** 12 columns.
  - Margins: 48 px at 1280 × 720, 96 px title-safe at 1920 × 1080, 64 px at 1080 × 1350 / 1080 × 1920.
  - Seams on column 5 or 7.
  - In vertical formats the seam rotates to horizontal.
- **Thumbnails (A / B / C):**
  - A: portrait 44 % + Field 56 %. B: the same, mirrored. C: a close cut-out on flat blue.
  - Headline 2–6 words, 130–180 px, max 2 lines. Keep both bottom corners free.
  - One Mark or the active season's gesture, never both. The count Tab is optional.
- **Video graphics:** Hossam is the anchor, and a graphic appears only when it does a job (YouTube Philosophy §6, §22). Overlays stay inside the title-safe area; cards hold for 4 s max. Everything is buildable in Premiere / CapCut.
  - *Talk set:* keyword (Mark), statement, question, quote (others' words = navy), chapter, lower thirds, comparison, framework.
  - *Evidence set:* source bar, document excerpt, table, statistic, diagram / workflow, screen-recording frame, definition. Numbers appear only with source, scope and year (§11; Distribution §27).
  - *Journey set:* **Revised** (a view updated from an earlier episode, §6), **Next question** end card (YouTube Philosophy §28), **Counterexample prompt** (Voice §31).
- **Motion:** `dur-fast` 120 ms, `dur-normal` 240 ms, `dur-emphasis` 420 ms.
  - Hard cuts, wipes from the seam, line-mask reveals.
  - The master emphasis move is the **Mark sweep**.
  - Banned: bounce, overshoot, 3D, glitch.
- **Short form:** 9:16, safe 240 top / 440 bottom / 140 right. Title frame for 0–1.2 s. Captions in Plex Arabic 700, 64 px.
- **Social:** native and episode-derived, with no quotas (Distribution §4, §16).
  - Master size is 1080 × 1350 with a 64 px margin.
  - Formats: key idea, framework, table, quote, insight, teaser, announcement.
- **Carousel:** question → case → diagnosis → framework → context → final thought.
- **Covers:** name, role, journey grid and publishing slot ("كل خميس · 8 م بتوقيت القاهرة", a V1 test per Distribution §36; edit it when it changes). No slogan.
- **Website:** "100 حلقة. كل أسبوع سؤال." This describes the project; it isn't a positioning claim.
  - Nav: الحلقات · المواسم · المصادر · عنّي.
  - Library first, no funnel (§19).
- **Iconography:** none. Use glyphs only: ■ ← ؟ 01 ”.

## 03 · Season layer

- **A season brings:** a name lockup in Thmanyah Sans, **one gesture** (built from rectangles and lines, in master colours, applied to one word per headline), an Sxx prefix, a row label on the grid, the opener's middle beat, a season cover and caption hashtags.
- **A season never:** adds a colour, font or logo, changes the photography, replaces the Field or Tab, or drops the journey count.
- **When a season ends:**
  - Its gesture retires from new work.
  - The archive keeps its thumbnails.
  - The next season's idea is decided by the editorial review (§25). Design follows.
- **Master opener:** black → the question → *season beat* → Field + name + count → cut to Hossam.

## 04 · Season 01 · تكسير المسلّمات (episodes 01–20)

- **Job:** establish the channel's intellectual behaviour (§14). The progression runs إنت بتبني إيه؟ → إيه اللي بايظ فعلًا؟ → إيه اللي بيحصل لما يكبر؟ → لو إنت جزء من المشكلة؟
- **Gesture:** **the Strike**, drawn through the *assumption* word only, in its broken form (0.13 em bar snapped with a 0.09 em step). Max one per piece.
  - Motion: draws right → left in 420 ms, then snaps.
  - Never used on master or extra content.
- **Markers:**
  - `S01 · 04 / 100` in the Tab.
  - `#تكسير_المسلمات` in captions only.
  - "مستوحاة من أحداث حقيقية" as an outline tag, only when true.
- **Naming flag:** the strategy files call this season **فكّ المسلّمات**. This system follows the brief's **تكسير المسلّمات**. If it changes, only this layer changes.

## Using the templates

`window.HG` (bundle.js):

- `HG.ab(w,h,scale,html)` makes an artboard.
- `HG.portrait({photo:'p1'|'p2'|'p3', w,h, head, top, cx, cut})` places a portrait by the head.
- `HG.thumb({tpl, photo, field, lines, tab})` builds a thumbnail. Put `<span class="mk">` on the Mark word. Use `<span class="s brk">` only in Season 01.
- `HG.journey({done, cell, gap, ground})` draws the 100 grid.
- `HG.assets.carouselCover / card / table / reel / ytBanner / fbCover / liCover / web / seasonCover / vg(kind)` build the rest.
  - `vg` kinds: keyword, statement, question, chapter, lower, stat, revised, next.
