01 — Master identity · photography

The same man, the same shirt, the same blue.

The photograph is the most recognisable asset in the system. The uniform is fixed — plain black crew-neck T-shirt, black trousers, black watch — and the world is fixed: a blue backdrop lit by a single hard beam. Faces are never retouched beyond dust and colour balance; never mirrored, never reshaped, never replaced.

Source set
Crops — placed by the head, not by the file
Photo + layout
  • Lighting one hard key from camera-left/high, a narrow blue beam across the backdrop, deep fall-off on the shirt. Shadows stay dark; no fill-flat beauty light.
  • Background seamless blue paper or wall lit with blue gel — always in the #0A1748 → #2047F4 → bright beam range. No rooms, offices, city skylines or bookshelves.
  • Contrast high. Blacks in the shirt can crush; skin must keep texture.
  • Skin natural, warm, real. Remove only temporary blemishes. No smoothing, no slimming, no eye enlarging.
  • Grade neutral skin, blues pushed toward #2047F4, blacks neutral (not blue). The optional mono treatment (grayscale + slight contrast) is for text-heavy covers only, and always sits on a blue Field.
  • Cut-outs allowed to place him on a flat blue Field (template C, covers). Always meet the bottom edge — he never floats.
  • Gaze to camera for thumbnails and profiles (trust, CTR). Looking off-frame toward the headline for editorial covers and articles.
  • Placement subject faces or looks INTO the Field. Never flip an image to make that work — shoot both sides.
  • Set bridge (flag) the V1 recording set is a home set with softboxes and warm practicals (Production §6–7), not the blue studio. Graphics are built to work over any background. A blue wall or paper backdrop behind the chair would join video to the photo world; that is an optional production upgrade, not assumed.
