02 — Content language · thumbnails

One architecture, three templates

Every thumbnail is a portrait + a solid Field + a 2–6 word Arabic headline set in the display face. The Field meets the portrait at a hard vertical seam at 56 / 44. The only metadata allowed is the Index Tab. No name, no logo, no hashtag: the face and the system are the signature. Titles are statements, not literal questions (a shared default taken from the Season 01 research). Sample copy comes from the Voice Guide.

Readability test — real YouTube sizes

Home feed (≈360 px), sidebar (168 px) and mobile search (246 px). If the headline can't be read at 168 px, it has too many words.

Safe zones · 1280 × 720
  • Margin 48 px (thumb-margin) on all sides. The headline never touches the seam: 40 px clearance.
  • Timestamp zone bottom-left 200 × 80 px (it sits bottom-right in LTR, but Arabic UI mirrors it). Keep both bottom corners free of words.
  • Headline baseline ends ≥ 92 px above the bottom edge.
  • Face — head height 44–62 % of the frame height, eyes in the upper 40 %. The face never crosses the seam.
  • Headline 130–180 px Arabic display, 900 weight, max 2 lines, 2–6 words. One word may carry the Mark (master) or the active season's gesture, never both.
  • Index Tab 24 px mono, top corner of the Field, journey count for core episodes (04 / 100); a season adds its prefix (S01 · 04 / 100); extra content has no Tab.
