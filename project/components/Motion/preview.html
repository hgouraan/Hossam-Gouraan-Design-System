02 — Content language · motion

Editing, not animating.

Motion behaves like a confident editor: hard cuts, straight wipes that start from the seam, text that unmasks line by line, and one slow, deliberate gesture: the Mark sweeping in behind the word that matters. Everything is achievable in Premiere or CapCut (Production §10, §23); After Effects is never required. Arabic moves right → left (reading direction). Everything eases with ease-snap cubic-bezier(0.2, 0, 0, 1). No bounce, no overshoot, no 3D, no motion blur, no glitch.
