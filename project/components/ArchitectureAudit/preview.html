00 — Architecture audit · V2

V1 made Season 01 look like the whole brand. V2 separates the two.

V1 was designed from the season brief, and it turned "breaking assumptions" into Hossam's permanent positioning, voice and main graphic gesture. The strategy documents say otherwise. The master brand is a long-term media asset built around a person and a 100-episode journey. The positioning is intentionally not written yet: "The answer should be earned through the body of work" (Master Strategy §27). Season 01 is one testable chapter (§26). V2 keeps every visual decision that is still valid and moves the rest into layers.

Flags for Hossam
1 · Season name. Master Strategy §14 and the Final Decision name Season 01 "فكّ المسلّمات". Your brief says "تكسير المسلّمات". V2 keeps your spelling. The two verbs differ: فكّ is untangling (investigative, closer to the editorial chain), while تكسير is smashing. The Strike fits تكسير. If the season is فكّ, the season gesture could become something that "unties" rather than crosses out. This is the only decision that can't be derived from the documents, and it only affects layer 04.
2 · Photo world vs recording set. Production Capabilities §6–7: home set, two softboxes (white/warm), warm practical lamp, no RGB. The portraits are blue-backdrop studio shots. The talking-head frame won't look like the thumbnails unless the set has a blue element. V2 designs video graphics to work over any background (solid Fields). A blue wall or paper backdrop behind the chair is suggested as an OPTIONAL PRODUCTION UPGRADE, not assumed.
3 · Schedule used on covers. Covers show "كل خميس · 8 م بتوقيت القاهرة" and launch 8 October 2026 (Distribution §36). That's a V1 test to review after 8–10 episodes, so treat the line as editable.
