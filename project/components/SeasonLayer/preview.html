03 — Season layer

A season is a chapter, not a rebrand.

Only the active season is planned in detail, and later seasons stay flexible (Master Strategy §13). So the system gives every season the same small slot. A season brings a name and one gesture; everything else is inherited. Below, the same piece in three states. The master layer alone is already complete.

A SEASON MAY DEFINE

  • Name + lockup in Thmanyah Sans (the season's only "logo").
  • One gesture applied to one word per headline. Built from rectangles and lines, in master colours.
  • Tab prefix Sxx · before the journey count.
  • Row label on the 100 grid.
  • Opener beat: the 1-second middle of the master opener.
  • Season cover on the master cover frame.
  • Hashtag / tags: captions only.

A SEASON MAY NEVER

  • Add a colour, gradient or tint outside the master tokens.
  • Add a font or switch the display face.
  • Change the photography world or uniform.
  • Replace the Field, move the Tab, or drop the journey count.
  • Touch the name signature or add a season logo-mark.
  • Run a second gesture next to its own.
  • Leave its gesture on master or extra content after the season ends.

WHEN A SEASON ENDS

  • Its episodes keep their thumbnails. The archive is not re-branded.
  • Its gesture retires from new work, unless a later season deliberately calls back to it.
  • The grid keeps its row, labelled, as part of the journey's history.
  • The review at the end of each season (§25) decides the next season's name and gesture; the design follows the editorial decision, not the reverse.
