01 — Master identity · name

The name is the mark.

No symbol. The signature is the Arabic name set in the display face, closed by the Square Stop — a solid blue square the size of an Arabic dot. The square is one unit of the journey: one episode, one question, one square in the 100 grid. It is not a "final word"; Hossam isn't positioned as someone with final answers (Master Strategy §6). It reappears as the bullet, the Tab square and the grid cell.

Primary
Arabic signature
حسام جعران + Square Stop. Display face, 900. Stop = 0.2 em, sits on the baseline, 0.07 em after the last letter. Website header, end cards, cover of every season, carousel final slide.
