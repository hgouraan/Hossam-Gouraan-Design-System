02 — Content language · short form

Vertical is its own layout.

Reels, Shorts and TikTok are not a shrunk YouTube frame. The seam rotates: Hossam in the top half (eyes at ~30 % height), a black Field rising from below for the title in the first second, then captions take over in the lower-middle band. Everything important stays inside the safe window: 240 px clear at the top, 440 px at the bottom, 140 px on the right (action buttons).

  • Title frame 0–1.2 s. Display 150–170 px, max 2 lines, black Field from 1000 px down. Index Tab at the top of the safe window.
  • Captions Plex Sans Arabic 700, 64 px, white, max 2 lines, 3–5 words per card. No outlines, no drop shadows: a solid black box behind the line when the background is busy.
  • Keyword one word per clip may jump to display 900 on a blue block — same as long-form A.
  • Series marker the Index Tab with the journey count (04 / 100), with a season prefix during a season. It stays for the first 3 s only; clips from extra content carry none.
  • End card 1.5 s, blue Field, the takeaway + signature. No "follow for more" stickers; the platform has its own button.
  • Cover uses the same title frame so the grid reads as one series (see Social → Reel covers).
