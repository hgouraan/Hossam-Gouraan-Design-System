02 — Content language · social

Episode-derived, not episode-dependent.

Social assets carry one strong idea from an episode in a form native to the platform. They aren't billboards (Distribution §4, §14), and there's no quota; an asset only exists if it has a reason. The master set covers the designed assets the strategy names: key idea, framework, table, quote, next-episode teaser, announcement (§12). 1080 × 1350 is the master size; stories and reel covers are 1080 × 1920 with the headline inside the central band.

  • Post margin 64 px. Tab top-right (journey count or card type), headline lower half, mono footer with @GOURAAN and the signature.
  • Quote = someone else's words (navy). Insight = Hossam's own line (black or blue). Table / framework = paper or navy, with a source line.
  • LinkedIn may lead in English. The same idea and evidence as the episode; only length, hook and wording change (§25).
