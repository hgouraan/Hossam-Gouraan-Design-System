04 — Season 01 · episodes 01–20

تكسير المسلّمات — the first chapter, not the brand.

Season 01's job is to establish the channel's intellectual behaviour (Master Strategy §14). It moves the viewer from "what is the correct thing to do?" to "why did I assume that was correct in the first place?". Its layer adds exactly three things on top of the master identity: the name lockup, the S01 · prefix, and one gesture: the Strike, drawn through the assumption, snapped in two in the season's own form. Take them away and what's left is Hossam Gouraan.

Naming flag: the strategy documents call this season فكّ المسلّمات; your brief says تكسير المسلّمات. This board uses your spelling. If it becomes فكّ, only this layer changes: the lockup and possibly the gesture (untying rather than crossing out).
