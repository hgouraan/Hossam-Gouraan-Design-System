01 — Master identity · graphic language

Four master shapes. All rectangles. No season in them.

Every master device is a plain rectangle, so none of them carries a season's idea. Seasons add one gesture on top (see the Season layer). Remove the gesture and these four still read as Hossam Gouraan.

PRIMARY · MASTER

The Field

  • Flat Electric Blue or Black block that holds the type, meeting the photograph at a hard vertical seam.
  • Splits 56/44 (default), 100 (text-led), 33/67 (portrait-led). Seams on a 12-column line.
  • Type sits on the Field, never on the face.
SECONDARY · MASTER

The Index Tab

  • Small black/blue block, square + mono label, top-right of the Field.
  • Master content = where you are in the journey: 04 / 100. Also carousel count (02 / 06), card type (TABLE, SOURCE, سؤال).
  • Seasons add a prefix (S01 · 04 / 100). Extra content outside the 100 (§21) carries no count.
SUPPORTING · MASTER · NEW IN V2

The Mark

  • A solid block behind the one word that carries the idea: the term being explained, the consequence, the real problem. Blue on black/paper, black on blue.
  • It highlights; it doesn't judge. Fits "experience first, label second" (Voice §8) and works for any season's topic.
  • Max one per piece. Also the in-video keyword device and the evidence highlighter on documents.
MICRO · MASTER · RE-MEANT

The Square unit

  • The blue square after the name is one unit of the journey: one episode, one question. V1 read it as "decided, final", which contradicts §6.
  • Squares build the 100 Journey grid (next board), the bullet, and the square inside the Tab.
  • Blue on black/paper; black on blue; white on navy.
