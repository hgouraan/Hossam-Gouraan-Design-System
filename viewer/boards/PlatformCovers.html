02 — Content language · platform identity

Covers say who, and where in the journey. Not a slogan.

V1 covers carried "أبني، أجرّب، وأسأل" and "question the obvious", which was Season 01 thinking dressed as permanent positioning. V2 covers carry only what the strategy supports: the name, the role (§6), the 100 Journey and the V1 publishing slot (Distribution §36; edit it when the slot changes). During a season, the cover can add a small season line, but it never replaces the master content. Everything important stays inside the smallest crop (dashed).
