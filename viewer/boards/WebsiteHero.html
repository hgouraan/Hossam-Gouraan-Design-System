02 — Content language · website

The home of a library, not a landing page.

The site exists so the body of work compounds: episodes, seasons and sources you can come back to when facing a decision (Master §18; Distribution §32). The hero describes the project factually (100 episodes, one question a week) and shows the journey grid. It doesn't claim a positioning the work hasn't earned yet. Navigation is episodes, seasons, sources, about. No funnel, no "Book a call" (§19: trust before offer).
