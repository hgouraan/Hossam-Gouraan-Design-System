01 — Master identity · colour

Three colours. Locked.

Electric Blue, Deep Navy and Black are fixed strategic decisions. Everything else is a tonal step of the blues, an off-black, white/off-white or a neutral grey. There is no functional accent — no red, green, orange, yellow or purple, including for states and charts. Richness comes from contrast, scale and tone.
