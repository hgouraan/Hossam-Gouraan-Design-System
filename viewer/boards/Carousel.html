02 — Content language · carousel

Six slides, one line of thought.

Read right to left. The rhythm follows the master editorial behaviour: question → case → diagnosis → framework → context → final thought. Grounds alternate (black, paper, blue, navy). Three things never move: the counter Tab at top-right, the 64 px start edge, and the mono footer. One Mark in the whole carousel; during a season, the season gesture may take the hook slide instead.

  • 01 Question black, display 130, one Mark. 02 Case paper, concrete situation first (Voice §9). 03 Diagnosis blue, one spoken sentence.
  • 04 Framework navy, numbered questions. 05 Context "it depends on…", no always/never (Voice §15). 06 Final thought black, one line + journey count + signature. No "Link in bio" by default.
