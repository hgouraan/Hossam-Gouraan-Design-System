02 — Content language · grid

Asymmetric by default.

Every artboard uses 12 columns with a proportional margin (≈ 5–6 % of the short side). Seams fall on column 5 or 7 — never 6 — so the page is never split in half. Headlines hang from the right margin (Arabic start edge) and sit in the lower half; metadata lives in the top band. Empty space is deliberate: at least a third of every Field stays empty.

Same content · three formats

The seam turns horizontal in vertical formats: portrait on top, Field below, headline in the lower-middle safe band. Nothing else changes — same type, same Tab, same Strike.

  • 1280 × 720 margin 48 · gutter 24 · seam col 7 (56 / 44)
  • 1920 × 1080 margin 96 (title-safe) · gutter 32 · seam col 5 or 7
  • 1080 × 1350 margin 64 · gutter 24 · seam col 5 / 7 or horizontal at 55 %
  • 1080 × 1920 margin 64 sides · safe top 240 · safe bottom 440 · right 140
  • Image / text ratio 44 / 56 (default), 33 / 67 (portrait-led), 0 / 100 (text-led).
  • Headline block max 8 of 12 columns; subtitle max 6; body max 5.
  • Top band (first 12 % of height) holds only the Index Tab and metadata.
  • Never centre a headline over a centred portrait. Tension comes from offset.
