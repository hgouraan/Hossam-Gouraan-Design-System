01 — Master identity · the 100 Journey

100 squares. One per week. It fills up.

The strategy's most permanent fact is the 100-episode weekly commitment: the audience's transformation, the creator's transformation, and a library that compounds (Master Strategy §2, §18). V1 had no picture of it. The grid is 5 rows × 20, matching the five 20-episode horizons in §12. It gets more meaningful every week, it's owned by no season, and it keeps working long after Season 01 ends.
