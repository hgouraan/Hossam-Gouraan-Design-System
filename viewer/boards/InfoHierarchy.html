15 — Iconography

No icon set. On purpose.

Icons would pull the brand toward corporate decks and SaaS. Photography, type and the four shapes carry the identity. Where a sign is needed, use a typographic glyph from the brand fonts — never a pictogram, never emoji. Platform icons (YouTube, LinkedIn…) appear only where a platform requires them, in white or black, never blue-tinted.
