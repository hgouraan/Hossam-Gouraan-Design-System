02 — Content language · video graphics

Hossam is the anchor. Graphics do a job, then leave.

The V1 format is Hossam as narrative anchor plus a supporting visual layer: tables, screenshots, documents, diagrams, sources, simple motion (YouTube Philosophy §6; Production §13). Every graphic below works over any background (solid Fields, no transparency tricks), can be built in Premiere or CapCut, and is season-neutral. Overlays stay inside the 96 px title-safe area and never cover the face. Cards replace the picture for up to 4 s. The argument must still work audio-only (§23).
